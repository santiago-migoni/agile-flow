# Retrospective

Read [the workflow map](../../AGENTS.md) and [the iterative lifecycle contract](../../references/iterative-lifecycle.md). Before writing, read [document operations](../../references/document-operations.md), [session protocol](../../references/session-protocol.md), and [authorization](../../references/authorization.md). Reuse sourced decisions and existing authorization. Resolve the product root; never edit the installed plugin cache.

Read [review and learning](../../references/review-and-learning.md). Separate process improvement from product evaluation. Record actual observations, a concrete adjustment, its owner or responsibility, revisit point and expected evidence using record-improvement and update-report for retrospective context. Keep product opportunities in backlog. Do not fabricate learning to fill a template or treat the retrospective as acceptance. At a sprint boundary, record a useful retrospective conclusion even when no adjustment is needed; then use the sprint closure procedure.
